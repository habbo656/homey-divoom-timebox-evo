# UAT v1.2.0: Additional features

**Status:** accepted 2026-11-15
**Build:** `main` @ `7f4440b` · **UAT issue:** #42 · **Issues:** #1 #2 #3 #4 #5 #6 #7 #19 #29

## Preconditions
- Build installed (see `docs/uat/README.md`: checkout the build commit, `npm ci && npm run build`,
  `scripts/homey-run.sh stop --uninstall`, `homey app install`), TimeBox Evo switched on, 1-2 m from the Homey, the Divoom
  phone app closed and the Evo not connected to any phone (it accepts one connection).
- A device paired with 1.1.0 is kept, no re-pairing. The app update adds the capabilities `volume_set`, `volume_mute` and
  "Audio playing" and switches the device class to `light` once at the start (breaking change of #19, see
  `docs/roadmap.md`, Breaking changes in 1.2.0). Existing Flows keep working: no id, setting or Flow card is removed.
- The app version in Homey > More > Apps still reads 1.1.0 with this build: the version bump ships after acceptance. That is
  not a failure.
- Homey app language English; repeat UAT-12 with German and switch back afterwards.
- A phone or computer that can stream audio to the Evo over Bluetooth (A2DP: pair it in the phone's Bluetooth settings, the
  Evo shows up as a speaker; UAT-4, UAT-5, UAT-10), a stopwatch, a webcam or your eyes on the Evo display, the Homey app
  (Devices, Moods, Flow editor with a button trigger or the "Test" button of the cards).
- Cases marked `[auto]` can be checked by the tester agent (`scripts/homey-api.sh` for cards, settings and capabilities,
  `scripts/homey-run.sh log` for the log, webcam via `scripts/hw-snap.sh`) in a `homey app run` (DEBUG) session. Hearing the
  speaker, streaming audio, the Homey app UI (Moods, tile, Flow editor), German texts and anything done on the device itself
  are owner-only.
- #1 (ESLint ignores `.hwtest/`) is a developer-tooling fix with nothing visible in the app and no test case (covered by
  `npm run lint` in the merge gate).

## Known open issues (not failures of this round)
- The volume, "Audio playing" and the audio triggers follow the Evo on the 60 s status check, so they can be up to about a minute
  late (UAT-4, UAT-5).
- After a sleep light has run out, the Evo switches itself off and stays unavailable in Homey until its power button is
  pressed (owner accepted 2026-11-15; UAT-9).
- A panel that is off may light itself in lighting colour for up to about a minute before the app turns it off again (#29, UAT-11);
  this only works while the app is connected.
- The reaction of the equalizer and noise meter to audio, the sleep light sounds and the volume scale are only judged by ear/eye.

## Test cases
### UAT-1 Equalizer mode on the tile and in the Flow cards (#2) [auto]
1. Device tile: open the mode picker and choose Equalizer.
2. Flow editor: run "Switch mode" with the mode Equalizer (after choosing Lighting first) with "Test". Add a Flow "When Mode changed"
   with a push notification.
3. Wait about 70 s, then run "Set temperature" `21` with "Test".
**Expected:** The mode picker offers Equalizer (en "Equalizer", de "Equalizer"). The Evo shows the spectrum bars (red peaks, low or
flat without audio) within about 2 s and the mode tile reads Equalizer. It still reads Equalizer after 70 s and after "Set temperature"
(the bars stay, no clock). "Mode changed" fires once for the switch from Lighting. The condition "Mode is ..." offers Equalizer.
`[auto]`: tile value `equalizer`, log `channel -> equalizer: 45 04 00` and `channel confirmed: equalizer`, no
`channel <- device: visualizer` and no `tile cleared`, photo shows bars.

### UAT-2 Equalizer and noise meter react to streamed audio (#2 #3)
1. Stream music from the phone/computer to the Evo (volume of the Evo above 0). Choose Equalizer on the tile.
2. Choose Noise meter on the tile. Make some noise near the Evo (talk, clap).
**Expected:** The Equalizer bars move to the music. The Noise meter shows the Evo's noise-meter view and reacts to sound; the mode
tile reads Noise meter and stays so after 70 s. Switching to Clock or Lighting leaves both views.

### UAT-3 Noise meter mode on the tile and in the Flow cards (#3) [auto]
1. Device tile: mode picker > Noise meter. Run the Flow action "Switch mode" > Noise meter with "Test".
2. Wait about 70 s. Then pick Clock.
**Expected:** The Evo shows the noise-meter view within about 2 s, the mode tile reads Noise meter and keeps it (no cleared tile after
the 60 s check). Clock shows the clock again. `[auto]`: log `channel -> noise_meter: 72 02 01`,
`channel confirmed: noise_meter (device reports channel code 0x..)`, no `channel mismatch` or `confirm timeout`, photo of the view.

### UAT-4 Volume and mute from Homey (#4) [auto: write and read-back]
1. Device tile: the volume slider and the mute button are shown. Play audio from the phone to the Evo.
2. Move the volume slider to about 20 %, then to 100 %, then to 7 %.
3. Press mute, then unmute. Set the slider to 0, then unmute.
**Expected:** The loudness follows the slider (audible; 100 % clearly louder than 7 %). Mute silences the Evo and the slider shows
0, unmute restores the last non-zero volume (not full volume; after "slider 0" it restores the volume before the 0). Slider 0 turns
mute on. No error toast. `[auto]` (tester, no audio): `volume_set` 0.2 logs `volume -> 3: 08 03` and `volume confirmed: 3`;
mute logs `mute -> on: 08 00`, unmute `mute -> off: 08 03`; `volume_set` 0 sets `volume_mute` true; no `08` value above `0f`.

### UAT-5 Volume and playing state follow the phone (#4)
1. Start audio playback from the phone to the Evo. Wait up to about 65 s.
2. Change the volume on the phone (slider) while it plays. Wait up to about 65 s.
3. Pause/stop the playback. Wait up to about 65 s.
4. Flow: "When Audio started playing" > push notification, "When Audio stopped playing" > push notification, and a Flow with
   the condition "Audio is playing" > push notification, run it while playing and while stopped.
**Expected:** The tile "Audio playing" turns on within about a minute of the start and off after the stop. The volume slider on the tile
follows the phone's volume within about a minute. "Audio started playing" and "Audio stopped playing" each fire once per change; the
condition is true only while playing. After an app restart or a reconnect the first read only updates the tile and fires no trigger.

### UAT-6 Notification icons (#5) [auto]
1. Flow editor: action "Show notification icon" with the dropdown. Run it with "Test" for several icons (at least Instagram, Facebook,
   WhatsApp, SMS, Incoming call, Missed call, Telegram) while the clock is shown.
2. Run it once while the tile is switched off.
**Expected:** The dropdown lists the 24 app names (en/de: brand names are identical, "Incoming call" / "Eingehender Anruf" and
"Missed call" / "Verpasster Anruf" are translated). The Evo shows the icon of the chosen app for about 2 s, then the previous view
returns by itself; the mode tile, colour and dim do not change. A panel that was off is turned on first. Report every icon that does
not match its name (it is then removed from the list). `[auto]`: log of the `50 TT` frame per icon, photo of each icon; the owner
judges the shown icon against the app name.

### UAT-7 Temperature unit setting and Flow unit (#7) [auto]
1. Device > Settings > group Weather: the setting "Temperature unit" (°C / °F). Look at its current value (an imperial Homey shows °F
   after the update, otherwise °C).
2. In the Clock settings switch "Show temperature" on and the mode to Clock. Flow "Set temperature" 21 with the unit "Device setting".
3. Change the setting to °F, wait a few seconds, watch the temperature page. Then run "Set temperature" 21 with the unit °C and then
   75 with the unit °F.
4. Set the setting back to your preferred unit.
**Expected:** The temperature page shows the label of the setting: 21 °C as `21 C`, as °F about `70 F` (the Evo converts, `5f` stays in
°C); the unit changes at once after saving the setting. "Set temperature" 21 °C shows 21 °C or the converted °F value of the device
setting, 75 °F shows 75 °F (a value in °F can differ by 1 °F because it is converted to whole °C). A Flow saved before 1.2.0 without
the unit keeps working with °C. A value outside the range of the chosen unit gives an error toast. `[auto]`: log
`temperature unit -> °F (setting): 2b 01`, photo of the page; the owner checks the first value after the update.

### UAT-8 Sleep light start, stop and end of the timer (#6) [auto: start, stop]
1. Flow editor: "Start sleep light", colour red, brightness 50 %, 5 minutes, sound "Phone audio", volume 10 %, "Test". Look at the Evo.
   (Use 5 minutes, you stop it again in step 3.)
2. Run "Stop sleep light" with "Test". Run "Stop sleep light" a second time.
3. Start the sleep light again with sound "Sound 1" (volume 30 %, 5 minutes), listen to the Evo and look at the panel.
   Then run "Stop sleep light".
**Expected:** 1. The whole panel lights red at medium brightness, no error toast, the tile stays on and the mode tile unchanged.
2. The first stop returns to the previous view (clock); the second stop succeeds silently (nothing running). 3. A sleep sound plays
(audible at the chosen volume, judge whether the volume is reasonable) and stops with "Stop sleep light". `[auto]` (tester, 5 min
timer, stop within 3 min): logs `sleep light started`, `sleep light confirmed`, `sleep light stopped`, `sleep light: none running`
and no `40` sent for the second stop.

### UAT-9 Sleep light ends by itself, power-off after the timer (#6)
1. Start the sleep light with the colour green, 1 minute, sound "Sound 1", volume 20 %, do not stop it.
2. Wait about 70 s and watch the Evo and the tile.
3. Press the power button of the Evo to switch it on again; wait up to 3 min.
**Expected:** The Evo plays the sound and shows green for about a minute, then switches itself off (dark, sound stops). In Homey the
tile turns unavailable after a short while (accepted behaviour). After pressing the button the Evo reconnects by itself within about
3 min and the tile works again.

### UAT-10 Sleep light and a display change from Homey (#6) [auto]
1. Start the sleep light (red, 5 minutes). Run the Flow "Set lighting" blue, then "Show text" `Hi`, then "Show clock".
2. Start it again and switch the tile off, then on. Then run "Stop sleep light".
**Expected:** Each display action ends the sleep light first (sound stops) and shows the requested view (blue, text, clock). Switching
the tile off only darkens the panel (the sleep light keeps running in the background); "Stop sleep light" afterwards returns to the
view under it (dark while off, the lit colour after power on). No error toast. `[auto]`: log `sleep light stop before lighting` /
`stop before text` and a photo of the requested view; log of the stop while off: panel dark, `onoff` false.

### UAT-11 Panel switched off stays off (#29) [auto]
1. Switch the tile off (the panel goes dark). Leave it for about 1 hour 15 minutes, across one full hour (for example from 12:45
   to 14:00). The Divoom app stays closed. Look at the panel every now and then.
**Expected:** The panel stays dark; if it lights itself in the lighting colour at some point, it is dark again within about a minute and
the tile switch stays off. The tile never flips to on by itself. Afterwards switching the tile on shows the clock/last mode. `[auto]`
(tester): log `woke while off (d8 01), sending off again` followed by `power -> off`, no `power <- device: on`, no `giving up`; if no wake
happens in the window, the result is inconclusive (unit tests cover the logic). Owner only: also try a lighting switch made in the
Divoom phone app while the tile is off (the app turns it off again).

### UAT-12 The TimeBox as a light in Homey moods (#19)
1. Homey app > Devices: look at the TimeBox tile and its icon/class (it is a light now). Open Moods > Add mood (or edit a mood) and
   add the TimeBox to a mood with a colour (for example blue), brightness 50 % and on.
2. Apply the mood. Then, with the clock shown, apply a green mood. Then apply a mood that turns the lights off.
3. Use "All lights off" for the zone (or the light group) the TimeBox is in.
4. German: repeat the mood selection with the Homey language German.
**Expected:** The TimeBox can be added to a mood. Applying the blue mood switches the Evo on and shows solid blue at about half
brightness; in clock mode the clock changes to green digits and the mode tile stays Clock; the off mood switches the Evo off.
"All lights off" switches the TimeBox off too. A mood cannot set the mode tile, clock style or card-only features. A device paired
before the update shows as a light after the app update without re-pairing. Report any mood that does not show the TimeBox.

## Edge cases
### UAT-13 Device unreachable (#2 #3 #4 #5 #6) [auto]
Precondition: the tile is on before the Evo goes away.
1. Switch the Evo off and wait about 30 s until the tile is unavailable.
2. Run "Switch mode" Equalizer, "Show notification icon" Telegram, "Start sleep light" and "Stop sleep light"; try the volume slider.
3. Switch the Evo on and wait up to 3 min.
**Expected:** 2. Each card fails at once with "The TimeBox is not connected right now. Try again in a moment." (de: "Die TimeBox ist gerade
nicht verbunden. Versuche es gleich noch einmal."), not with a hang; the slider fails with the same text or Homey shows its own
unavailable message. 3. The tile recovers by itself, the volume and "Audio playing" read the device again and the same actions work.

### UAT-14 Invalid input (#5 #6 #7) [auto]
1. "Start sleep light" with minutes 0 and 241, brightness 0 and 101, an empty colour. "Set temperature" 250 °C unit °C and 300 unit °F.
   In the Flow editor the number fields stop most of this; the tester sends the values with `homey-api.sh`.
**Expected:** Each invalid value fails with a clear localised error naming the field (en and de, no raw key), nothing is sent to the
Evo and the display does not change.

### UAT-15 German texts (#2 #4 #5 #6 #7)
1. Set the Homey app language to German. Look at the mode picker, the new Flow cards (Show notification icon, Start sleep light,
   Stop sleep light, the audio triggers and condition), the capability "Audio läuft" and the setting "Temperatureinheit".
**Expected:** All new texts are German ("Benachrichtigungssymbol anzeigen", "Starte Schlaflicht", "Audio läuft", "Lärmmesser",
"Equalizer"), no English fallback or raw key. Switch back to English.

### UAT-16 Restart the app, state stays consistent (#4 #7 #19)
1. Homey app > More > Apps > Divoom TimeBox Evo > Restart app (exact entry name varies). Time it.
2. Open the tile and the settings.
**Expected:** The device stays paired and available again within about 3 min, volume, mute and "Audio playing" match the Evo, the class
is still a light (moods still list it), the temperature unit setting is kept and the actions work. `[auto]` part: one
`TimeBox Evo app initialized` per start in the log, no `device class -> light` or `could not` line after the first start; the real
restart is owner-only.

## Regression smoke (accepted milestones)
- Pairing (v0.2.0): the tile is available within ~45 s with on/off, brightness, colour, mode, and now volume, mute and "Audio playing".
- Availability (v0.2.0): switch the Evo off for 1 min and on again; "TimeBox disconnected"/"TimeBox connected" fire once each.
- Time (v0.2.0): the clock shows Homey's local time.
- Mode picker and clock (v0.3.0): Clock, Lighting, Custom switch within ~2 s; "Clock style" changes the clock at once.
- Show text (v0.4.0): `Hello Homey` scrolls; "Stop text" ends it.
- Weather (v0.4.0): "Set weather" Snow shows snow with "Show weather" on; automatic weather still updates.
- Countdown (v0.4.0, v0.5.0): 0:20 shows the hourglass and ends with the end display; "Countdown ended" fires once.
- Images (v0.5.0): "Show image from URL" with a PNG shows a recognisable 16x16 mosaic within ~8 s.
- Release (v1.0.0): app and driver names, pairing hints, stability (power cycle, out of range, Homey restart) recover by themselves.
- Bugfixes (v1.1.0): healthy actions succeed without error toast, a dim while off is held until power on, a countdown end lights a
  panel that was off and the tile follows, the app icon shows in the "+" list.
