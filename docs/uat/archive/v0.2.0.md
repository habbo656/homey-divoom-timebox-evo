# UAT v0.2.0: Core rebuild

**Status:** accepted 2026-10-06
**Build:** `main` @ `2f9018c` · **UAT issue:** #54 · **Issues:** #18 #19 #20 #21 #22 #23 #46

## Preconditions
- Build installed (see `docs/uat/README.md`), Timebox Evo switched on, 1-2 m from the Homey, the Divoom phone app closed
  and the Evo not connected to any phone (it accepts one connection).
- No Timebox device paired yet (remove old ones). Homey app language English; repeat UAT-12 with German.
- A webcam or your eyes on the Evo display. Homey time zone set correctly (Homey settings).
- Cases marked `[auto]` can be driven by the tester agent (`scripts/homey-api.sh`, `devLink` app setting, webcam via
  `scripts/hw-snap.sh`) in a `homey app run` (DEBUG) session; `devLink` exists only in such a run, never in the installed
  build. Pairing (UAT-2, UAT-3) is always by hand in the Homey app. Cases without the mark are manual.

## Known open issues (not failures of this round)
- #49 Brightness: `dim` is acknowledged and read back (`46`), but the panel may not visibly change until the Evo has been
  rebooted (power-cycled). If UAT-6 shows no visible change, power-cycle the Evo once and repeat; report what you saw.
  The Homey tile value must still follow.
- #48 Transport hardening (stale BLE connection after a very unlucky reconnect, unvalidated timing options): no visible
  behaviour expected in normal use.
- Pairing window: closing it asks for confirmation. This is Homey client behaviour (documented in `docs/architecture.md`).
- Only `dim`, availability and time are implemented; there is no on/off, text, clock style or channel yet (later milestones).

## Test cases
### UAT-1 App installs and starts (#18)
1. Install the build (`homey app install`), open Homey app > More > Apps.
**Expected:** "Divoom Timebox" is listed, no crash or error notification, no app settings page (device first).

### UAT-2 Pair the Timebox Evo (#22, #46)
1. Homey app > Devices > "+" > Divoom Timebox.
2. Read the first page, press Next.
3. Watch the search page for the countdown; wait for your Timebox to appear (name like `TimeBox-Evo-audio` / `-light`).
4. Select it and press Add.
**Expected:** First page shows the instructions (switch on, close Divoom app, reset hint, stay awake, search takes up to
60 s). Search page shows "Searching for Bluetooth devices, N s left..." counting down from 60 and the list fills while it
runs. Only Timebox-like devices are listed. After Add: "Checking the device..." with spinner, then the window closes and a
"Timebox Evo" tile appears with a brightness slider, available (not greyed out) within ~45 s.

### UAT-3 Pairing: rescan, show all, nothing found (#46)
1. Start pairing again with the Evo switched OFF.
2. Wait for the countdown to end.
3. Press "Search again" after "Search finished."; switch the Evo on and wait.
4. If a "Show all devices" link is shown, press it, then "Show only Timebox devices".
5. Pick your Evo (it is not offered twice if already paired) or close the window.
**Expected:** With the Evo off: after 60 s the text "No Timebox found. Switch it on, keep it close and awake, then
search again. ..." and a "Search again" button. After "Search again" and with the Evo on, the Evo appears. "Show all
devices" lists further named Bluetooth devices; "Show only Timebox devices" hides them again. An already paired Evo is not
listed. Closing asks for a confirmation (known, see above).

### UAT-4 Wrong device is rejected (#46, #22)
1. Pair again, press "Show all devices", pick a non-Timebox device (e.g. a headset), press Add.
**Expected:** After a few seconds "This is not a Timebox Evo. Pick another device or search again." (or the
"did not answer" text), controls are usable again, no device is created.

### UAT-5 Device tile reflects the connection (#22)
1. Open the device tile after pairing.
**Expected:** Tile shows the brightness slider and no "unavailable"/warning banner. Brightness matches the Evo's current
brightness (read from the device, not a default).

### UAT-6 Change brightness from Homey (#22) [auto]
Steps (tester: `scripts/homey-api.sh devices set-capability-value --device-id <id> --capability-id dim --value 0.2`, then `1`, photo with
`hw-snap.sh` after each; log via `homey-run.sh log`):
1. Move the slider on the device tile to about 20 %, then to 100 %.
**Expected:** Tile follows without an error toast. App log: `[device] dim confirmed: 20` and `... confirmed: 100`
(`dim mismatch` is a finding). Panel gets visibly dimmer/brighter (see #49: if not, power-cycle the Evo and repeat). Value 0
turns the panel dark (known device behaviour).

### UAT-7 Brightness changed on the device is read back (#22) [auto]
1. Change the brightness on the Evo itself (touch/button), or tester: `devLink` value `brightness:30`.
2. Wait up to one poll interval (60 s by default).
**Expected:** The Homey tile slider moves to the new value without touching it; log `[device] dim <- device: B`.

### UAT-8 Availability Flow cards (#22)
1. Flow editor > create a Flow: WHEN "Timebox connected" (Timebox Evo) THEN send yourself a notification; a second Flow
   WHEN "Timebox disconnected" THEN notification; a third: IF "Timebox is connected" THEN notification, run from the Flow
   editor test button.
2. Switch the Evo off; wait about 60 s.
3. Switch it on; wait up to 3 min.
**Expected:** Cards exist in English and the condition reads "Timebox is connected" / "... is not connected" when
inverted. "Timebox disconnected" fires once, only after the Evo was unreachable for about 30 s, and the tile turns
unavailable with "The Timebox does not respond. Switch it on, keep it within range and close the Divoom app." After
switching on, the tile becomes available again and "Timebox connected" fires once. The condition is true while connected
and false after the 30 s grace period without the Evo.

### UAT-9 Availability via simulated drop (#21, #22) [auto]
Steps (tester, DEBUG run, device paired and available): write `devLink` `drop#1`; watch log and tile state; wait for
`[link] connected` and `[device] available`; repeat with `drop#2`.
**Expected:** Log shows `[link] ... disconnected`, a reconnect with backoff and `[device] available` again. A drop that
recovers within 30 s causes no `unavailable` and no trigger (no flapping).

### UAT-10 Time sync on connect (#23) [auto]
Steps (tester: `devLink` `time:200101010101` to set a wrong clock, `calendar` to show date, photo; then `drop#1` and wait for
the reconnect; `calendar` again, photo):
1. With the setting "Set the time automatically" on (default), set the Evo clock wrong (or note it), then power-cycle
   the Evo (or let the link drop and reconnect).
2. Show the clock/calendar view on the Evo (the clock channel with date).
**Expected:** After the reconnect log shows `[device] time synced (connect): YYYY-MM-DD hh:mm:ss <zone> UTC+hh:mm` and
the Evo shows the correct time, date and weekday of your Homey's local time zone (also correct around midnight and in
summer time).

### UAT-11 Time sync setting and Flow action (#23) [auto]
1. Device settings (wrench icon) > "Clock": uncheck "Set the time automatically", save. Make the Evo clock wrong
   (`devLink` `time:200101010101`) and reconnect (`drop#1`).
2. Check the Evo time and the log: no `time synced (connect)`.
3. Flow editor: THEN "Sync time now" (Timebox Evo) and press the test button.
4. Check "Set the time automatically" again and save.
**Expected:** With the setting off the clock stays wrong after reconnect. The Flow action sets the correct time and date
at once even with the setting off (`time synced (flow)`), no error. Switching the setting on while connected syncs at
once (`time synced (setting)`). The hint texts under the setting are readable in the app. With the Evo off, the action
fails in the Flow editor with "The Timebox is not connected right now. Try again in a moment."

### UAT-12 German texts (#22, #23, #46)
1. Set the Homey app language to German; open the pairing window, device settings, the Flow cards.
**Expected:** All pairing, settings ("Uhr", "Uhrzeit automatisch stellen", "Erweitert", "Intervall der Statusabfrage") and
Flow card texts ("Timebox verbunden", "Timebox getrennt", "Uhrzeit jetzt synchronisieren") are German; no raw keys.

### UAT-13 Status check interval setting (#22)
1. Device settings > Advanced > "Status check interval": enter 10, then 100000, then 45, save each.
**Expected:** 10 and 100000 are rejected with "The status check interval must be a whole number of seconds between 30 and
3600." (or the setting refuses the input); 45 is saved, the device stays available.

### UAT-14 Power-cycle the Evo (manual, roadmap risk, #21 #22 #23)
1. Device available, Flows from UAT-8 active, "Set the time automatically" on, Evo clock visible.
2. Unplug the Evo power (and remove battery if any) for 10 s; watch the Homey tile.
3. Plug it in again and wait; note the time until the tile is available.
**Expected:** Within ~3 min of replugging the tile is available again without any action in the app, "Timebox connected"
fires once, brightness slider shows the device value, and the clock shows the correct time (`time synced (connect)`).
During the outage "Timebox disconnected" fired once after about 30 s. After this reboot also note whether brightness
changes are visible (#49).

### UAT-15 Out of range and long run (manual)
1. Carry the Evo out of Bluetooth range (or switch off) for 5 min, bring it back.
2. Leave everything alone for 30 min.
**Expected:** Tile turns unavailable then recovers on its own after the Evo is back in range; no repeated
connected/disconnected triggers during the idle half hour (one Flow notification per real change only).

## Edge cases
### UAT-16 Device unreachable: actions fail readably (#22, #23)
1. Switch the Evo off, wait until the tile is unavailable.
2. Try the brightness slider and run "Sync time now".
**Expected:** No hang. The tile is unavailable (slider not usable) and the Flow action reports "The Timebox is not
connected right now. Try again in a moment."

### UAT-17 Other Bluetooth client holds the Evo (#22, #46)
1. Connect the Divoom phone app to the Evo, then try pairing / watch the tile.
**Expected:** Pairing: "The device did not answer. Close the Divoom app, make sure the Timebox is on and awake, then try
again." Existing device: unavailable with the "does not respond ... close the Divoom app" text; recovers after the phone
disconnects.

### UAT-18 Remove and re-pair (#22, #46)
1. Remove the device in Homey, pair it again.
**Expected:** The Evo is found again and pairs without a power cycle; the new tile works as in UAT-5/UAT-6.
