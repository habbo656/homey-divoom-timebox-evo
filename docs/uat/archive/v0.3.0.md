# UAT v0.3.0: Display & modes

**Status:** accepted 2026-10-07
**Build:** `main` @ `13c2e26` · **UAT issue:** #83 · **Issues:** #24 #25 #26 #27 #63 #68 #69 #70 #78 #85 #88

## Preconditions
- Build installed (see `docs/uat/README.md`), Timebox Evo switched on, 1-2 m from the Homey, the Divoom phone app closed
  and the Evo not connected to any phone (it accepts one connection).
- A device paired with the v0.2.0 build (before 0.3.0) is ideal for UAT-1 (migration). Keep it if you still have it, install
  the 0.3.0 build over it and do not re-pair until UAT-1 is done. Otherwise pair a new one (UAT-1 then only checks the
  capabilities of a fresh device). Homey app language English; repeat UAT-22 with German.
- A webcam or your eyes on the Evo display. Homey time zone set correctly.
- Cases marked `[auto]` can be driven by the tester agent (`scripts/homey-api.sh devices set-capability-value`, Flow cards run via the
  API, webcam via `scripts/hw-snap.sh`) in a `homey app run` (DEBUG) session. Pairing is always by hand. Cases without the
  mark are manual (Flow editor UI, texts, Homey-wide actions, buttons on the device).
- Colours and animations are judged by eye; the exact look of the clock styles may differ from the descriptions.

## Not tested by hand
- #63 (prettier formatting enforced in the merge gate) is tooling only and has no user-visible behaviour; no UAT case.

## Known open issues (not failures of this round)
- #58 The Evo ignores the 12/24 h flag (it always shows 24 h). The setting was removed in #70; the clock is always sent as 24 h.
- #59 The clock rotates through animation pages (heart, duck, ghost, cat) even with weather, temperature and calendar
  switched off. Expected, not a failure.
- #62 Changing the brightness (`dim`) while the panel is off lights the panel again; the on/off tile may stay "off" until
  the next status check. Listed as known, not a failure.
- #49 (from v0.2.0) The brightness may not visibly change until the Evo has been power-cycled once.
- "Set lighting" with black is accepted, but the Evo does not go dark: it shows a pale white (log `lighting mismatch:
  device #FFFFFF, requested #000000`). Seen in the pre-check 2026-10-07; to turn the light off use the on/off tile.
- Only the modes "Clock", "Lighting" and "Custom" are offered (cloud, visualizations and the scoreboard were removed, #68).
  When the device shows cloud or a visualizer (operated on the device), the mode tile is empty. For text, games or alarms
  on the device the tile stays at its last value.
- With the Analog round clock style the Evo itself draws a pale-blue ring and hands around the dial whatever colour is
  sent (seen in the #85 hardware test). Not a failure; judge the colour on the digits/dial only.
- Choosing the small clock style on the device itself while it shows another mode may show the round dial (#88): the app
  sends nothing in that case, so it cannot correct it. The same holds if the device left the clock by itself and the app
  has not noticed yet (up to 60 s until the next status poll). Not a failure.
- Setting the mode picker to "Clock" while the clock is already shown counts as "Switch mode Clock" and brings back the
  clock style configured in the settings (it replaces the style of a "Show clock" run).
- Scoreboard limitation (#68): the scoreboard never changes the status the app reads, so a scoreboard shown on the device
  (for example via the Divoom app) is invisible to Homey: the tile keeps the previous mode.

## Test cases
**Round 2 (after round 1 was rejected on UAT-10):** #85 is fixed: power on now restores the clock face shown by "Show
clock" (style and colour) instead of falling back to the "Clock style" setting. Please re-test UAT-9 and UAT-10 fully
(including step 4), plus UAT-20 and UAT-24, which were not reported in round 1. Then a short smoke of UAT-5, 6, 11 and
12, which the change touched (clock face on power on, tile colour in clock mode). All other cases were not affected.
#88 is fixed as well: "Show clock" with "Small" from the lighting mode or from off showed the round dial instead of the
small digits. Please re-check UAT-9 "Small" from lighting and from off.

### UAT-1 Existing device gets the new capabilities (#24, #26)
1. With the device paired before 0.3.0 and the 0.3.0 build installed (restart the app if needed), open the device tile.
**Expected:** Without re-pairing the tile shows a power button, brightness slider, colour control and a "Mode" picker with
three entries. No "unavailable" banner beyond the normal start-up time (up to ~45 s). A device paired fresh shows the same.
A clock colour that was set as the old "Clock colour" setting is taken over once (log `clock colour migrated from the setting: #RRGGBB`;
a fresh device logs `clock colour initialised: #FFFFFF`); the setting no longer exists.

### UAT-2 Mode picker offers the three modes (#24, #68) [auto]
1. Open the "Mode" picker on the tile.
**Expected:** Exactly the entries "Clock", "Lighting", "Custom" (German: picker title "Modus", entries "Uhr", "Beleuchtung",
"Benutzerdefiniert"); no Cloud, Visualizer or Scoreboard. The current mode of the Evo is selected (read from the
device, not a default).

### UAT-3 Switch to each mode from the tile (#24, #68) [auto]
Steps (tester: `scripts/homey-api.sh devices set-capability-value --device-id <id> --capability-id timebox_channel --value <id>` for
`clock`, `lighting`, `custom`, photo after each).
1. Select "Clock", then "Lighting", "Custom", one after the other.
**Expected:** The Evo changes within about 2 s each time and the tile shows the selected entry without an error toast:
Clock = clock face (as set in UAT-5); Lighting = single-colour light; Custom = a user page of the device. Log `[device] channel confirmed: <id>`.

### UAT-4 Mode changed on the Evo is shown in Homey (#24, #68)
1. Switch the mode with the buttons/touch on the Evo (e.g. from clock to custom), wait up to one poll (60 s).
**Expected:** The tile picker moves to the new mode without touching it. Text, games or alarms on the device leave the tile
unchanged. (Cloud and visualizer: UAT-15.)

### UAT-5 Clock settings: style, and the clock colour from the tile (#25, #69) [auto]
1. Device settings (wrench) > "Clock" (there is no colour setting any more). Select the clock mode on the tile first.
2. Set "Clock style" to each of: Full screen, Rainbow, Boxed, Analog square, Full screen negative, Analog round, Small; save
   after each. For two styles also pick a colour on the tile colour control (red, then green) while the clock is shown.
**Expected:** While the clock is displayed the Evo switches to the style at once (otherwise at the next clock selection).
Styles look different from each other (large digits / rainbow bars / frame / square dial / negative / round dial / small
one-line digits); the tile colour changes the clock digits (boxed: digits only; analog_square may show the colour only
faintly, not a failure), the mode picker stays "Clock" (it does not change to "Lighting") and the style and page flags stay.
The setting texts and hints are readable. Logs `[device] clock settings applied`, `colour for clock: #FF0000`.

### UAT-6 Clock colour is kept per mode (#69) [auto]
1. Select "Clock", set the tile colour to red. Select "Lighting", set the tile colour to blue. Select "Clock" again, then
   "Lighting" again. Select "Custom", then set a colour on the tile.
**Expected:** Each mode comes back in its own colour (clock red, lighting blue) and the tile colour control shows the colour of
the mode that is shown. Setting a colour while "Custom" is shown switches to "Lighting" in that colour (the Flow trigger
"Mode changed" fires).

### UAT-7 Clock settings: weather, temperature, calendar flags (#25) [auto]
1. With the clock mode shown, switch on "Show calendar" only, save; watch about 1 min. Repeat with "Show weather" only
   and "Show temperature" only, then all off.
**Expected:** Calendar: the clock rotates to a red date page (weekday and `MM-DD`). Weather: a weather page. Temperature: a
thermometer page with a number and `C`. All off: the clock still rotates through animations (#59, expected). The Evo
shows the time page at times in every setting.

### UAT-8 Settings list (#70)
1. Open the device settings.
**Expected:** Only these settings are shown: Clock style, Show weather, Show temperature, Show calendar, Set the time
automatically (de: Stil der Uhr, Wetter anzeigen, Temperatur anzeigen, Kalender anzeigen, Uhrzeit automatisch stellen). No
"24-hour clock", no "Advanced" group, no "Status check interval". The hint of "Set the time automatically" does not mention a Flow action.

### UAT-9 Flow action "Show clock" (#25) [auto]
1. Flow editor: THEN "Show clock" (Timebox Evo), pick style "Rainbow" and a colour, press the test button. Repeat with
   "Small" and another colour while the Evo shows the lighting mode and while the panel is off.
**Expected:** The Evo shows the clock in that style and colour at once; the colour stays the clock colour (the tile colour
control shows it, the next clock selection uses it). The style stays as well (#85): turning the Timebox off and on, and a
colour change on the tile, show the same style; it stays until the clock is selected again (tile or "Switch mode") or a
clock setting is saved (then the style of the settings is shown). The setting "Clock style" itself is not changed. From the
lighting mode and from off the panel switches on and shows the clock. With "Small" from lighting or from off the Evo may
show the round dial for about 1.5 s, then the small digits (#88: the app sends the clock command a second time; log `clock face re-sent (small from lighting|off)`). The tile mode picker shows "Clock", the on/off tile shows on. The card is named "Show
clock" (de: "Uhr anzeigen"). The hint does not mention 12/24 hours (#78).

### UAT-10 Power off and on, restore of the last mode (#26, #68) [auto]
Steps (tester: `set-capability-value ... onoff false` / `true`, photo each).
1. Select "Custom" on the tile. Press the power button off.
2. Press it on.
3. Repeat with "Clock" as last mode and with "Lighting" (set a colour first, UAT-12).
4. (#85) Set "Clock style" to Analog round, select "Lighting" (green), run "Show clock" in full screen and red, wait 1 min,
   power off, wait 1 min, power on. Then select "Lighting", then "Clock" on the tile, power off and on again.
**Expected:** Off: the panel goes dark, tile shows off and keeps the mode picker at the previous mode. On: the Evo
returns to the mode from before (custom / clock with its style / lighting in the same colour). Log `power -> off` and
the `channel -> <id>` on switching on. Step 4: after the first power on the Evo shows the red full-screen clock (not the
round dial; log `clock face confirmed: style full_screen colour #FF0000`, no `colour for clock` line); after selecting
"Clock" on the tile the round dial in red shows, and power off and on shows it again. The setting stays "Analog round".

### UAT-15 Device-side switch to cloud (#68) [auto]
Steps (tester: `homey app run` session, `scripts/homey-api.sh apps set-app-setting --id com.habbo656.divoom-timebox --name devLink --value 'channel:2'`
for cloud and `'channel:0'` for the clock; the device log shows the lines below).
1. Flow: WHEN "Mode changed" (Timebox Evo) THEN send a notification. Show the clock on the Evo (tile "Clock").
2. Switch the Evo to its cloud gallery on the device (touch/buttons; tester: `channel:2`). Wait up to one poll (60 s).
3. Switch the Evo back to the clock on the device (tester: `channel:0`). Wait up to one poll.
4. Repeat step 2, then power the Timebox off and on with the tile power button.
**Expected:** Step 2: the Evo shows the cloud gallery, the mode picker on the tile becomes empty (no entry selected), no
notification arrives, and a Flow condition "Mode is Clock" is false (also for Lighting and Custom). Log
`channel <- device: cloud (mode not offered), tile cleared`, once. Step 3: the picker shows "Clock" again and exactly one
notification arrives (`clock`). Step 4: the empty picker as in step 2; after power off and on the Evo shows the clock (the
last offered mode), the picker shows "Clock", and one "Mode changed" notification arrives. A visualizer shown on the
device behaves like the cloud (`04`). No error toast at any step.

## Edge cases
### UAT-23 Device unreachable: display actions fail readably (#24 #25 #26 #27)
1. Switch the Evo off, wait until the tile is unavailable (about 30 s).
2. Run "Switch mode", "Show clock", "Set lighting", "Show custom page" from the Flow editor.
**Expected:** No hang; each card fails with "The Timebox is not connected right now. Try again in a moment." The tile is
unavailable; after switching the Evo on it recovers by itself and shows its state (mode, colour, brightness).

### UAT-24 Invalid input (#25 #26 #27)
1. Flow "Show clock" or "Set lighting" with a colour from a Flow tag that is not a valid colour (if you can produce one).
**Expected:** A readable error text, nothing is sent to the Evo, the current display stays.

### UAT-25 Quick successive changes (#24 #27)
1. Run "Show custom page 3" and immediately "Show clock" (two Flow test presses within a second) and tap two modes
   quickly on the tile.
**Expected:** The newest change wins and the tile shows the same mode as the Evo after a few seconds; no error toast.

## Regression smoke (v0.2.0, accepted)
- UAT-2 pairing (v0.2.0): pair, list, Add, tile available within ~45 s with brightness, now also on/off, colour and mode.
- UAT-6 brightness: slider changes the Evo brightness, tile follows without error toast.
- UAT-7/8 availability: switch the Evo off for 1 min and on again; "Timebox disconnected"/"connected" fire once each, tile
  becomes unavailable and recovers by itself.
- UAT-10/11 time sync: with "Set the time automatically" on, wrong clock is corrected after reconnect (the Flow action "Sync time now" was
  removed in #70).
- UAT-14 power-cycle (unplug the Evo for 10 s): tile available again within ~3 min, now also with the correct mode and on/off state.
