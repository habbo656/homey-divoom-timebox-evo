# UAT v1.1.0: Bugfix after Release 1.0

**Status:** accepted 2026-10-18
**Build:** `main` @ `766eb05` · **UAT issue:** #164 · **Issues:** #147 #141 #125 #48 #62 #60 #129 #94 #151

## Preconditions
- Build installed (see `docs/uat/README.md`: checkout the build commit, `npm ci && npm run build`,
  `scripts/homey-run.sh stop --uninstall`, `homey app install`), TimeBox Evo switched on, 1-2 m from the Homey, the Divoom
  phone app closed and the Evo not connected to any phone (it accepts one connection).
- A device paired with 1.0.0 is fine and is kept: 1.1.0 changes no id, capability, setting, Flow card or argument, so no
  re-pairing is needed. UAT-1 needs the "Add new device" list (you do not have to add a second device).
- Homey app language English; repeat UAT-2 with German and switch back afterwards.
- The app version in Homey > More > Apps > TimeBox Evo still reads 1.0.0 with this build: the version bump ships after
  acceptance (`docs/uat/archive/v1.0.0.md`, Preconditions). That is not a failure.
- A webcam or your eyes on the Evo display, a stopwatch (UAT-5 waits about 20 s, UAT-10 and UAT-11 up to about 3 min, UAT-6 and UAT-7
  none), access to the Homey app on your phone (More > Apps for the app list) and a Flow editor with a button trigger (or the
  "Test" button of the cards), used in UAT-2 to UAT-8 and UAT-10.
- Cases marked `[auto]` can be checked by the tester agent (`scripts/homey-api.sh` for cards, settings, capabilities and the
  dev commands, `scripts/homey-run.sh log` for the log, webcam via `scripts/hw-snap.sh`) in a `homey app run` (DEBUG) session.
  Seeing the app icon in the Homey app, the Homey app UI, German texts and anything done on the device itself are owner-only.
- #48 (stale BLE connection, timing options) and #94 (`scripts/homey-run.sh` without arguments) have nothing visible in the
  Homey app and no test case; they are covered by the unit tests and the hardware tests of the pipeline. #129 (native 16x16
  images) is also unchanged for users and only pinned by tests, UAT-8 is a quick visual check.

## Known open issues (not failures of this round)
- The "did not confirm" error (UAT-9) needs a link that is muted but not yet dead, which a real Evo almost never produces; it
  is checked by the tester with the dev command `mute` (`docs/testing/stability.md`, S4b and S4c). You only check that healthy actions do not
  fail by mistake (UAT-3).
- The limits of text, weather, countdown and images (`docs/roadmap.md`, Known limitations) still apply.

## Test cases
### UAT-1 App icon in "Add new device" and in the app list (#147)
1. Homey app > Devices > "+". Look at the app entry "TimeBox Evo" in the list of brands/apps (search "TimeBox" if needed).
2. Open the app entry and look at the driver "TimeBox Evo". Cancel the pairing.
3. Homey app > More > Apps: look at the entry "TimeBox Evo". Check in light and dark mode.
4. The App Store page can only be checked after you publish 1.1.0: a local install does not change the store page (it shows the
   last published version, 1.0.0). A miss on the store page in this round is not a #147 failure; check it after publishing.
**Expected:** The pixel fox app icon is shown on the app entry and in the app list, and in the "+" list; no empty square or
placeholder. The driver icon (Evo line drawing) is shown for the driver. It is not verified whether Devices > "+" uses the local
or the store-hosted icon: if the icon is missing only there, report it with that caveat. If the app icon is still missing, say where
(list "+", app list) and in which mode; #147 stays open then. If the icon was visible before this build, say so.

### UAT-2 Mode change on a healthy link works and reports success (#141) [auto]
1. On the device tile pick the modes Lighting, Custom, Clock one after the other with the mode picker.
2. Flow editor: run the actions "Show clock" (style and colour set), "Set lighting" (colour `#00FF00`) and "Show custom page" (page 1)
   with "Test".
**Expected:** Every action turns green (no error) and the Evo shows the requested view within about 2 s (clock, green light, custom
page); the mode picker follows the Evo. No "did not confirm" text appears. Owner-only: repeat with the Homey language German (no error either).

### UAT-3 Healthy-link actions succeed: dim, power, text, countdown, image (#141 #151) [auto]
1. Tile: move the brightness slider to 40 %, then 80 %. Switch the tile off, then on.
2. Flow "Show custom page" 2, then "Show text" `Hello` (duration `10`), "Start countdown" 0 min 20 s and "Stop countdown",
   "Show image from URL" with a PNG address Homey can reach.
**Expected:** None of them shows an error toast or a red Flow result. The brightness changes at once and stays at the slider value,
the panel goes dark when switched off and returns to the last mode when switched on (brightness as before), the text scrolls, the
countdown shows the hourglass and ends with the Stop, the image appears as a 16x16 mosaic within about 8 s.

### UAT-4 Dim while the panel is off is held until power on (#62) [auto]
1. Switch the tile off (the Evo display goes dark). Move the brightness slider to 20 %.
2. Check the panel: it stays dark. Switch the tile on again.
3. Repeat with the Flow: switch off, set the brightness with the tile to 70 %, then run "Show clock" (the display command switches the
   panel on).
**Expected:** 1./2. The panel does not light up while the slider moves; the tile keeps the 20 % value. After switching on the Evo shows
the last mode at 20 % brightness (not at the old brightness, not at full brightness). 3. The "Show clock" lights the panel and it
shows the clock at 70 %. No error toast.

### UAT-5 Countdown end lights a panel that was off (#125) [auto]
1. Start with the clock shown (Flow "Show clock"). Flow "Start countdown" 0 min 20 s. Wait 1 s, then switch the tile off (the display goes dark; the countdown is only hidden).
   Note the last lighting colour of the tile.
2. Wait for the end of the 20 s (about 20 s). Watch the Evo and the tile.
3. Optional: a Flow "When Countdown ended" > push notification "Countdown ended", and a Flow "When Mode changed" > push notification.
**Expected:** At the end the Evo lights up and briefly shows the end display (hourglass/end frame), then stays lit in the last lighting
colour (not the clock). The tile switch flips to on by itself (no manual action), the mode tile reads Lighting and the colour tile shows
that colour. "Mode changed" fires (lighting; only because the mode before was not Lighting) and the optional notification of "Countdown ended" arrives once. Without the countdown the
panel stays off (`docs/protocol/countdown.md`, P12).

### UAT-6 Clock settings save during a switch to the clock (#60) [auto]
1. Set the mode to Lighting (green). Open the device > Settings.
2. Switch the mode picker to Clock and change "Clock style" (for example from Full screen to Rainbow) right after it (or the style
   first, then the picker). By hand the race window is under a second and hard to hit: the race itself is checked by the tester with
   parallel `scripts/homey-api.sh` calls (the setting and the capability at the same time). The owner step only checks that there is
   no false failure.
3. Change "Show temperature" on and off once while the clock is shown.
**Expected:** The settings save without an error. The Evo shows the clock with the new style (Rainbow) and the changed pages, no old
style flashes back after a moment and the mode tile reads Clock.

### UAT-7 Device settings and Flow cards unchanged (#141 #151 no breaking change) [auto]
1. Owner-only: open the Flow editor of an existing Flow from 1.0.0 that uses TimeBox cards, and the device settings.
   (`[auto]`: the cards and settings listed by `homey api` are unchanged.)
**Expected:** The Flows have no "unknown card" or missing argument warning and still run; the device keeps its name, tile and settings.
The cards and settings are those of the 1.0.0 list (triggers "TimeBox connected", "TimeBox disconnected", "Mode changed", "Countdown ended").

### UAT-8 Native 16x16 image is shown unchanged (#129) [auto]
1. Flow "Show image from URL" with a native 16x16 PNG without transparency (for example the `native16.png`
   pattern of `docs/protocol/images.md`, Native 16x16 image: red/blue checkerboard, white/black columns, grey and green ramps, served from
   any web server Homey can reach; the file is not in the repository, create it from that description; transparent pixels would show black) and the fit "Whole image (black bars)".
2. Compare with the source image.
**Expected:** The Evo shows the image pixel for pixel: distinct colours and sharp 1-px edges (the checkerboard stays a checkerboard), no blur, no black bars, no shifted rows or columns.

### UAT-9 Unconfirmed change fails with "did not confirm" (#141 #151) [auto]
Not practical to provoke by hand: the error needs a link that is silent but still counted as connected (the first up to about 2 min
after the Evo stops answering). The tester provokes it with the dev command `mute` (`docs/testing/stability.md`, S4b and S4c); owner
cases only see the "not connected" error of UAT-10.
1. (Tester) With the link up run "Show clock" (green). Run `mute`, then within 10 s "Show clock" (red), set the mode picker to Lighting, set
   `dim` to 30 %, run "Show custom page" 1 and turn the tile off, back to back. Read the log. An action that overlaps `link dead`
   correctly fails with `not_connected` instead; that is allowed.
2. (Tester) Wait for `link dead` and the reconnect, then run the same actions again.
**Expected:** While muted each action fails after about 5 s (the custom page after about 10 s) with exactly "The TimeBox did not confirm the change.
Check the display and try again in a moment." (de: "Die TimeBox hat die Änderung nicht bestätigt. Prüfe die Anzeige und versuche es gleich noch
einmal."); the log shows `channel confirm timeout`, `dim confirm timeout` or `power off confirm timeout`, never `error: null` together with a confirm timeout, no raw text or key,
no `Unhandled`/`TypeError`. The tile reads the requested value. After the reconnect every action succeeds again.

## Edge cases
### UAT-10 Device unreachable (#141 #151) [auto]
Precondition: the tile is on before the Evo goes away.
1. Switch the Evo off and wait about 30 s until the tile is unavailable.
2. Run the Flow cards "Show clock", "Show text" `Hello` and "Start countdown" 0:20, and try the brightness slider. Homey may block the
   slider of an unavailable device and show its unavailable message instead; the tester sets `dim` via `homey-api.sh`.
3. Switch the Evo on again and wait up to 3 min.
**Expected:** 2. Each Flow card fails at once with "The TimeBox is not connected right now. Try again in a moment." (de: "Die TimeBox ist gerade
nicht verbunden. Versuche es gleich noch einmal."), not with the "did not confirm" text and without a hang. A `dim` change while the tile is off is held without an error (#62), so
test it with the tile on: it fails with the same "not connected" text, or Homey shows its own unavailable message; the "did not confirm" text or a hang is a failure. 3. The tile recovers by itself
and the same actions succeed.

### UAT-11 Restart the app, state stays consistent (#48 #62 #125)
1. Homey app > More > Apps > TimeBox Evo > Restart app (exact entry name varies). Time it.
2. Open the tile; run "Show clock" and "Show text" `Hello`.
**Expected:** The device stays paired, is available again within about 3 min, tile (brightness, colour, mode, on/off) matches the Evo, and the
actions of step 2 work without errors. Nothing is stuck in a half-state. `[auto]` part: exactly one `TimeBox Evo app initialized` per start
in the log; the real restart is owner-only.

### UAT-12 German texts (#141 #151)
1. Set the Homey app language to German. Run an action while the Evo is off (UAT-10).
**Expected:** The error reads "Die TimeBox ist gerade nicht verbunden. Versuche es gleich noch einmal.". The Flow cards and settings are
German as in 1.0.0. Switch back to English.

### UAT-13 Dim and mode changes in quick succession (#62 #141) [auto]
1. Move the brightness slider several times within 2 s while the clock is shown, then pick Lighting and back to Clock.
**Expected:** No error toast; the Evo ends at the last slider value in the last picked mode and the tile shows the same.

## Regression smoke (v0.2.0 to v1.0.0, accepted)
- Pairing (v0.2.0): the tile is available within ~45 s with on/off, brightness, colour and mode.
- Availability (v0.2.0): switch the Evo off for 1 min and on again; "TimeBox disconnected"/"TimeBox connected" fire once each, the tile recovers by itself.
- Time (v0.2.0): the clock shows Homey's local time.
- Mode picker and clock (v0.3.0): Clock, Lighting, Custom switch within ~2 s; "Clock style" changes the clock at once.
- Show text (v0.4.0): `Hello Homey`, duration `0` scrolls; "Stop text" ends it.
- Weather (v0.4.0): "Set temperature" `21` shows 21 °C with "Show temperature" on; "Set weather" Snow shows snow with "Show weather" on.
- Countdown (v0.4.0, v0.5.0): 0:20 shows the hourglass and ends with the end display; "Countdown ended" fires once and not after "Stop countdown".
- Images (v0.5.0): "Show image from URL" with a PNG shows a recognisable 16x16 mosaic within ~8 s.
- Release (v1.0.0): app and driver are named "TimeBox Evo" (en and de), pairing hints and Flow card titles are as in 1.0.0, stability cases of v1.0.0 (power cycle, out of range, Homey restart) still recover by themselves.
