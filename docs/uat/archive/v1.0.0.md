# UAT v1.0.0: App Store release

**Status:** accepted 2026-10-16 (round 2) · round 1 rejected → bug #142 (fixed)
**Build:** `main` @ `6764e41` (round 2; round 1: `faf4b1e`) · **UAT issue:** #140 · **Issues:** #92 #36 #37 #38 #133 #142

## Round 2 focus
Round 1 passed except UAT-1 (driver icon too thin, #142). Only these are tested again:
- **UAT-1** (priority): the driver icon is redrawn with bold lines (40 / 20 / 10 px). Compare it next to other device icons in the
  driver list, in light and dark mode. Please also say whether you **accept** two intentional design choices: the deeper
  three-quarter view of the Evo and the 8x8 pixel grid on the display.
- A **quick regression smoke**: UAT-3 (pair, tile) and UAT-18 (existing device and Flows after the update), then the "Regression smoke" section at the end.
- All other cases passed in round 1 and are not repeated unless you report otherwise.
- **Install:** the dev build is uninstalled. Check out `main` @ `6764e41`, `npm ci && npm run build`, `homey app install`, and pair
  the Evo again if the device is gone (see Preconditions).

## Preconditions
- Build installed (see `docs/uat/README.md`: checkout the build commit, `npm ci && npm run build`,
  `scripts/homey-run.sh stop --uninstall`, `homey app install`), TimeBox Evo switched on, 1-2 m from the Homey, the Divoom
  phone app closed and the Evo not connected to any phone (it accepts one connection).
- A device paired with an earlier version (0.2.0 to 0.5.0) is fine and is kept: no re-pairing is needed for 1.0.0, ids and Flows
  are unchanged. UAT-1 needs a second pairing (add the Evo again as a new device, or remove and re-add it); keep one device when done.
- Homey app language English; repeat UAT-6, UAT-7 and UAT-9 with German and switch back afterwards.
- A webcam or your eyes on the Evo display, a stopwatch (UAT-10 to UAT-15 wait up to 3 min), a phone with Bluetooth and an audio
  app (UAT-14), access to the Homey (power cycle and restart for UAT-12 and UAT-13) and the Homey app on your phone.
- A test Flow with a button trigger (or the "Test" button of the cards in the Flow editor) for "Show text", "Show clock" and "Show
  image from URL" (any PNG address Homey can reach).
- Cases marked `[auto]` can be checked by the tester agent (`scripts/homey-api.sh` for cards, settings and manifest,
  `scripts/homey-run.sh log` for the log, webcam via `scripts/hw-snap.sh`) in a `homey app run` (DEBUG) session. Pairing, the
  Homey app UI, texts in German, phone/power/restart actions and anything done on the device itself are owner-only.
- The release process (#38: version consistency, changelog, tag) is not visible in the Homey app and has no test case. After
  acceptance the version bump `chore(release): v1.0.0` ships (`docs/versioning.md`); the app version in Homey > More > Apps >
  TimeBox Evo then reads 1.0.0 after the next install.

## Known open issues (not failures of this round)
- Stability (#36, GitHub issue #141): during the up to about 2 min between a muted link and its dead-link detection, an action such as "Show clock"
  can take about 5 s and report success although the Evo did not confirm it (by design, `docs/architecture/modes.md`).
- Stability (#36): "Show image from URL" with an unresolvable host can take up to about 70 s until the error shows.
- Names (#133): devices that were paired before keep their old name (for example "Divoom Timebox"); only the driver name, cards and
  texts changed. Rename the device in Homey if you like.
- The limits of text, weather, countdown, images (v0.4.0, v0.5.0) still apply (`docs/roadmap.md`, Known limitations).

### UAT-10 Evo power cycle (#36, M1)
1. Unplug the Evo (and switch it off if it has a battery) for 10 s, then plug it in and switch it on again. Watch the tile and the
   display. Time it.
2. Run "Show text" `Hello` (duration `10`) once the tile is available.
**Expected:** After about 30 s the tile shows the device as unavailable with "The TimeBox does not respond. Switch it on, keep it
within range and close the Divoom app." Within about 3 min of switching on, the tile is available again by itself (no re-pairing,
no app restart), shows the clock, the brightness and colour of the tile, and the text runs. "TimeBox disconnected" and "TimeBox
connected" fire once each (see UAT-16).

### UAT-11 Evo out of range (#36, M2)
1. Take the Evo out of Bluetooth range (another floor or wrap it in metal foil for the test) for at least 2 min. Watch the tile.
2. Bring it back within 1-2 m.
**Expected:** After the grace period of about 30 s the tile is unavailable with the "does not respond" text above; no error toasts
and no stuck state. After returning, the device is available again within about 3 min without any action and accepts "Show text".

### UAT-12 Homey restart (#36, M3)
1. Homey app > More > Settings > System > Restart Homey (or power-cycle the Homey). Wait until it is online.
2. Open the device tile.
**Expected:** The app starts by itself, the device reconnects without re-pairing and is available again (the Evo shows the clock or
the last mode; brightness as before). Flows that use the device still work (run "Show text"). This takes a few minutes after
Homey is online.

### UAT-16 Connection Flows around an outage (#36 #133)
1. Create Flows: "When TimeBox disconnected" > push notification "TimeBox lost"; "When TimeBox connected" > push notification "TimeBox

2. Switch the Evo off for 2 min, run the third Flow; switch it on again, wait, run it again.
**Expected:** "TimeBox lost" arrives once about 30 s after the Evo goes away, "TimeBox back" once after it returns (also after UAT-10 to
UAT-12). The condition takes the else branch while the Evo is off and "OK" scrolls once it is back.

## Regression smoke (v0.2.0 to v0.5.0, accepted)
- Pairing (v0.2.0): tile available within ~45 s with on/off, brightness, colour and mode.
- Availability (v0.2.0): switch the Evo off for 1 min and on again; "TimeBox disconnected"/"TimeBox connected" fire once each, the tile recovers by itself.
- Time (v0.2.0): the clock shows Homey's local time; with "Set the time automatically" on, a wrong Evo time is corrected after reconnecting.
- Brightness slider and on/off tile follow the Evo without an error toast; switching on returns to the last mode.
- Mode picker (v0.3.0): Clock, Lighting, Custom switch the Evo within ~2 s; "Show clock" with a style and colour, "Set lighting", "Show custom page" work.
- Clock settings (v0.3.0): changing "Clock style" while the clock is shown updates the Evo at once; no animation pages unless "Show animation pages" is on.
- Show text (v0.4.0): "Show text" `Hello Homey`, duration `0` scrolls; "Stop text" ends it and the mode is shown.
- Weather (v0.4.0): with "Show temperature" on, "Set temperature" `21` shows 21 °C; "Set weather" Snow shows snow.
- Countdown (v0.4.0): "Start countdown" 0:20 shows the hourglass and ends with the end display; "Stop countdown" returns to the previous view.
- Images (v0.5.0): "Show image from URL" with a PNG shows a recognisable 16x16 mosaic within ~8 s; "Whole image (black bars)" keeps the aspect ratio.
- Countdown ended (v0.5.0): a Flow "When Countdown ended" fires once at the end of a 0:20 countdown and not after "Stop countdown".
