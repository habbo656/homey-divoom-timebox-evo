# Countdown evidence

_Evidence for [divoom-protocol.md](../divoom-protocol.md) section 5.1 and 5.6; opcode rows and status stay there._

## Opcode table notes

### 5.1 Countdown row

| Payload | Function | Reply | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `72 03 OO MM SS` | countdown: OO `01` start / `00` stop, MM minutes (0..99), SS seconds (0..59) | `72 55 03`; unsolicited `71 55 03 01 01 00` when it ends | VERIFIED (Mac probe 2026-10-07, #33, section 5.6). An **overlay**, not a channel: `46` d0 / d8 are unchanged while it runs. Replaces a running native text. Ends by itself and returns to the previous view; a stop returns to the clock instead. Builders `startCountdown` / `stopCountdown` |

## 5.6 Countdown: evidence

Mac BLE probe (`scripts/probe/ble.py`, `hex:` steps, nothing else holding the link), webcam photos every 2 s from a
background `imagesnap -t 2` timelapse (`.hwtest/agent_issue-33-countdown/`, gitignored), `46` polled every 3..6 s. Start
`72 03 01 MM SS`, stop `72 03 00 00 00`. The frames below are the reply bytes after the SPP header.

| # | Question | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Start 0:40 from the clock (`72 03 01 00 28`) | Reply `72 55 03` within 1 s. The panel shows an hourglass icon on the left, minutes (blue) on top and seconds (red) below, counting down once per second (photos: `00` / `36`, `00` / `22`, `00` / `08`, `00` / `02`). `46` d0 stays `00` (clock) and d8 stays `01` for the whole countdown: **the countdown is invisible to `46`**, so it is an overlay and no mode code appears (no escalation). |
| P2 | Behaviour at the end (clock, 0:40) | At 00:00 the panel shows `00:00` with the colours swapped (minutes red, seconds blue) and the hourglass for about 4 s, then an empty hourglass, then the panel is dark for about 8 s and the clock rotation returns. **Unsolicited frames at the end (research answer: yes, the Evo reports it):** `71 55 03 01 01 00` arrives together with the end (at the end time, within the polling interval, in every run), followed by `bd 55 13 01 32 00` right after it and `bd 55 13 01 1e 00` about 12 s later (meaning of `bd 13` unknown). Control: 80 s of polling without a countdown showed none of them. No beep could be checked (webcam only). |
| P2b | End from lighting red (0:10) | After the `00:00` display the panel returns to the lighting colour (red) after about 3 s without a dark phase; `46` d0 stays `01` throughout. **The natural end restores the previous view.** |
| P3 | Stop (`72 03 00 00 00`) while it runs | Reply `72 55 03`. The countdown digits disappear (empty hourglass for about 4 s, then dark), and **the device switches to the clock channel by itself 6..9 s after the stop (`46` d0 `01` -> `00`, still `01` 3 s after it)**, observed from lighting red (5:00) and lighting green (1:00). **A stop does not restore the previous view.** Sending the mode again right after the stop (here `45 01 00ff00 …` 1 s after the stop) wins: lighting stayed, d0 `01` for 32 s, no countdown remnant. |
| P4 | Stop with nothing running (lighting blue) | Acknowledged with `72 55 03`, nothing changes (panel and d0 `01` unchanged for 21 s). Harmless. |
| P5 | Range | `72 03 01 63 3b` (99:59) and `72 03 01 3b 3b` (59:59) count down correctly (`99:56`, `59:57` in the photos). `72 03 01 64 00` (minutes 100) shows `99:57` after 3 s, so the device clamps to 99:59 (the app never sends it). `72 03 01 00 00` (0:00) ends at once: `71 55 03 01 01 00` within 1 s and the end sequence of P2. A truncated `72 03 01` (no minutes, no seconds) was acknowledged and showed the garbage `36:45` (the next bytes of the frame): the device does not validate, the app must. |
| P6 | Mode change, power off and on during a countdown | A channel command (`45 01 ff0000 32 00 01 000000`) shows the new channel at once, **but the countdown keeps running hidden**: at its end the `71 55 03 01 01 00` frame arrives and the panel flashes the `00:00` end display for about 4 s before returning to the channel (0:20 started from the clock, lighting sent after 4 s, end display at 20..24 s). Power off (`45 01 … 00`) darkens the panel, a power on (`45 01 … 01`) shows lighting, not the countdown, and the countdown still ends on time (0:30 run). **With the panel off at the end, the end display lights the panel** (`46` d8 `00` -> `01`) and it stays lit afterwards (lighting red). |
| P6b | Stop while the countdown is hidden | **A stop is ignored unless the countdown is on screen:** with the countdown hidden behind a lighting channel (panel on) or while the panel was off, the reply was `72 55 03` but the countdown still ended on time (`71` frame, end display, d8 `01`). `46` stayed d0 `01` / d8 unchanged, nothing else happened. |
| P7 | Start while the panel is off (lighting off, 0:12) | The countdown is shown and the panel is on: `46` d8 `00` -> `01` within 1 s, d0 stays `01`. After the end the panel stays lit with the lighting colour (red); it does not go back to off. |
| P8a | Countdown while a native text runs | The countdown replaces the text at once. After the end the clock is shown, the text does not come back (`46` d0 `00`). |
| P8b | Text during a countdown, then `6e 00` | The text replaces the countdown. After `6e 00` the clock is shown, the countdown is not restored, **but it keeps running hidden** and its end display appears at the original end time (`71` frame at 40 s, photo `00:00` swapped colours). |
| P9 | Start again while one runs (0:20, 3 s later 0:08) | The second start replaces the first: the display shows `00:06` a second later, the end display comes 8 s after the second start and there is **no second end** at the first countdown's time. |
| P10 | `5f 17 01` (weather) during a countdown (0:30) | Not disturbed: it kept counting (`00:22` at 8 s, `00:10`, `00:06`) and ended on time (`71` frame at 30 s). |
