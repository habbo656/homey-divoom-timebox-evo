# Display opcode table notes

_Evidence for [divoom-protocol.md](../divoom-protocol.md) section 5.1 and 5.4; opcode rows and status stay there._

## Opcode table notes

### 5.1 Display and system rows

| Payload | Function | Reply | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `74 BB` | brightness 0..100 | `32 55 BB`, `46 …` | VERIFIED = acked and stored (read back in `46` d6/d10). **The visible effect is unresolved** (#49): on the owner's Evo 1..100 showed no visible difference, only 0 turned the panel off (clock view, lighting channel white, and with the brightness byte of `45 01 RRGGBB BB 00 01 000000`). See section 11 |
| `45 01 RR GG BB BB TT PP 00 00 00` | lighting: colour, brightness, TT effect, PP power | `45 55 01` | VERIFIED. TT: 0 plain colour, 1 love, 2 plants, 3 no-mosquito, 4 sleeping; 5–8 show only the plain colour. **The app always sends TT `00` since #69** (the effects were removed from the app, the protocol layer keeps the byte). **Effects on Homey (#26 hardware test, photos):** 1 love with `#FF00FF` is animated (magenta/pink with a bright centre, then blue); 4 sleeping with `#FF00FF` showed orange-red, so effects seem to use their own palette and may ignore RR GG BB (not confirmed for every effect). PP `00` turns the panel off (the dark panel is the only observable effect; the Evo sends `45 55 01` and an unsolicited `31`/`46`). **Power-off behaviour (probe 2026-10-06, #26, section 3):** `46` d0 is `01` after the off command; `46` d8 follows the power byte of the last `45` command (`00` after off, `01` after any channel command) but not `74`; `46` does not wake the panel; **`74 BB` does wake it** (the lighting colour comes back lit); every channel command (`45 00` full form, `45 02`, `45 04 NN`, `45 05`, `45 06`, `45 01 … 01`) relights the panel |
| `45 04 NN` | visualization NN | `45 55 04` | VERIFIED: 12 distinct visualizations 0–11 (spectrum bars, faces, figures, …), react to streamed audio (6.1); 12–14 look like 1. Homey Pro 2026-10-06 (#27, photos, without audio; 1-based as in the Flow dropdown, index = N-1): 1 bottom spectrum bars with red peaks, 2 cartoon face with blue eyes and teeth, 3 centred rainbow waveform, 4 blue-haired monster face with red eyes, 5 sparse green twinkling dots, 6 green tiki mask face, 7 mirrored side spectrum bars, 8 purple robot face, 9 scattered blue/green sparkles, 10 red-blue stick figure on the ground, 11 cyan arrow/fish shape, 12 framed picture with an orange border |
| `45 06` | scoreboard view | `45 55 06` | VERIFIED (the panel shows the scoreboard). **`46` d0 is not updated by `45 06`**: the status frame after `45 06` was byte-identical to the one before (from custom `46 55 05 …` before and after; from the clock d0 stayed `00`), observed on the Homey Pro 2026-10-06 (#24). The scoreboard cannot be confirmed or detected through `46`. `45 07` ignored |
| `18 YL YH MO DD hh mm ss WD` | set date/time (year = YH*100+YL, WD 0=Sun) | `18 55` | VERIFIED: the calendar showed the right weekday. **Changes the device clock**. Homey Pro 2026-10-06 (#23): `18 55` seen after every set (connect, dev `time:`, Flow, reconnect); Homey's Node Intl resolved Europe/Berlin UTC+02:00 correctly (no small-icu problem); the re-sync after a forced drop arrived ~6 s after the drop |
| `5f TT WW` | weather: temperature (signed), type 1 clear, 3 cloudy, 5 thunder, 6 rain, 8 snow, 9 fog | none | VERIFIED: snow icon shown in the clock cycle. Builder `setWeather` (#30): the temperature is one signed byte (two's complement, -128..127), the app limits it to -99..99; negative temperatures are encoded as `5f fb 08` for -5 °C snow. Hardware (#30, 2026-10-07, clock `full_screen` with weather and temperature pages on): `5f fb 08` (-5 snow) and `5f f4 05` (-12 thunder) show a minus bar left of the digits, so the byte is two's-complement int8. `5f 18 01` (24), `5f 0c 06` (12), `5f 07 09` (7) and `5f 17 03` (23) show the number with ° and C. The clock rotates clock face, weather icon (animated), clock face, temperature page (thermometer on cyan, black digits). Icons photographed: 1 clear (green landscape with a yellow flower/sun), 5 thunder (cloud, lightning, rain), 8 snow (snowflakes); 3, 6 and 9 were accepted (temperature page shown) but their icons were not photographed. `5f` changed neither the mode nor the power state |

### 5.4 Persistent settings rows

| Payload | Function | Reply / read-back | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `43 II OO hh mm WD MM TT FL FH VV` | alarm slot II (0..9): on, time, weekdays bitmask (bit0=Sun, `7f` = every day), mode, trigger, FM, volume | `43 55`; `42` returns the 10-byte record unchanged | VERIFIED: at hh:mm a full-screen animation (dancing figure) plays for ~30–40 s, then the previous view returns. `46` d18 = `01` afterwards. Alarm off = `43 II 00 …` |
| `54 SS OO MO DD hh mm 01 + 32 B title` | memorial slot SS (0..9): on, date/time, title as 16 UTF-16BE chars (zero-padded) | `54 55`; `53` returns the record including the title | VERIFIED (read-back). Fired at the same minute as a test alarm, so its own display is not separated; the animation stopped when the memorial was cleared. Clear = `54 SS 00 01 01 00 00 00 + 32×00` |
| `bd 17 PP` / `bd 17 ff` | select custom page 0..2 (after `45 05`) / read it | read → `bd 55 17 PP` | VERIFIED: three different pages shown. Homey Pro 2026-10-06 (#27): after `45 05` (ack `45 55 05`) `bd 17 PP` is applied and acknowledged with `bd 55 17 PP 3x 01` (bytes after PP not decoded); the read `bd 17 ff` returns the current page. Pages 1–3 (PP 0..2) show the three custom pages set up in the Divoom app (owner's content): a green window with three buttons, a white cat/dog face with red corners, a blue background with a heart |
